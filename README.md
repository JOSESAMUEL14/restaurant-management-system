## ✨ Features

### 🎨 UI/UX Features
- ✅ **Hero Carousel** - Auto-sliding banners with call-to-action
- ✅ **Delivery Bar** - Delivery info with icons (Swiggy style)
- ✅ **Animated Stats Counter** - Numbers animate on scroll
- ✅ **"What's on your Mind?"** - Food categories carousel with real images (Swiggy style)
- ✅ **Quick Filters** - Mood-based filtering (Family, Romantic, Pure Veg, Buffet, Premium)
- ✅ **Popular Chains** - Restaurant cards with discounts (Zomato style)
- ✅ **Gourmet Privileges & Combos** - Offer cards with badges
- ✅ **The Gastronomic Compendium** - Full menu with search & filters
- ✅ **Signature Creations** - Chef's special picks
- ✅ **Customer Reviews** - Testimonial carousel
- ✅ **Table Booking** - Interactive table map
- ✅ **Shopping Cart** - Add/remove items with real-time totals
- ✅ **Payment System** - Cash, Card, and Online payment options
- ✅ **Order Tracking** - Live order status with animation
- ✅ **Bill/Receipt Generation** - Printable invoices
- ✅ **Dark/Light Theme** - Toggle between themes

### 🛠️ Admin Panel
- ✅ **Dashboard** - Sales analytics and KPIs
- ✅ **Chains Manager** - Add/Edit/Delete popular chains
- ✅ **Categories Manager** - Add/Edit/Delete categories
- ✅ **Menu Manager** - Add/Edit/Delete menu items
- ✅ **Order Queue** - View and manage orders
- ✅ **Salon Status** - Table management
